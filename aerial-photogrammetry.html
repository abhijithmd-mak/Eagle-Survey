Aerial Photogrammetry

Aerial Photogrammetry uses overlapping high-resolution photographs and geospatial processing to produce georeferenced orthomosaics and 3D site models. Eagle scopes each collection around the capture area, accuracy requirements, and the outputs the project team needs.

What It Is

From Overlapping Images to Measurable Site Models.

Aerial Photogrammetry reconstructs three-dimensional geometry from overlapping high-resolution photographs. Specialized software identifies common features among the images and creates a georeferenced record that can be viewed, measured, compared, and incorporated into engineering and construction workflows.

Before mobilization, Eagle establishes the image coverage, flight altitude, airspace, georeferencing, and delivery requirements. The photographs are then aligned, processed, reviewed, and published through DroneDeploy or delivered in the agreed file formats.

Image Coverage

Planned overlap creates consistent coverage across the capture area.

Geospatial Processing

Overlapping photographs are aligned and converted into georeferenced mapping products.

DroneDeploy Review

Authorized users can view, measure, share, and coordinate from the processed imagery.

Best-Fit Conditions

Where Aerial Photogrammetry Adds the Most Value.

Open or Lightly Vegetated Sites

High-resolution mapping where the ground and visible surface features can be clearly photographed from the air.

Commercial & Industrial Developments

Georeferenced aerial mapping supporting due diligence, design coordination, construction, and documentation.

Construction Progress & Recurring Documentation

Recurring photogrammetry can support Eagle’s Construction Progress Monitoring and Documentation service by creating a consistent visual record over time.

Large Tracts, Subdivisions & Campuses

Efficient coverage across broad project areas requiring detailed visual context and surface information.

Earthwork & Stockpile Surfaces

Photogrammetric surface models can support earthwork and stockpile analysis when those calculations are included in the project scope.

Transportation, Utility & Site Corridors

Aerial imagery and surface coverage along visible roadway, utility, drainage, and site-development corridors.

Aerial LiDAR generally provides a higher degree of accuracy than aerial photogrammetry, which is one of the factors Eagle weighs when recommending a collection method. When a project requires both high-resolution visual context and bare-earth terrain beneath dense vegetation, Eagle can coordinate Aerial Photogrammetry and Aerial LiDAR Mapping within the same scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Project Teams Ask About Aerial Photogrammetry.

How accurate is drone photogrammetry?

Accuracy requirements are established during scoping and carried through flight planning, project control, image quality, calibrated equipment, processing, and quality review. Eagle develops the control and QA/QC approach around the intended use of the finished products.

What is an orthomosaic?

An orthomosaic is a continuous, georeferenced aerial image assembled from overlapping photographs and corrected so it can be viewed at a consistent scale. It provides a detailed overhead record that can support measurement, planning, design coordination, construction documentation, and stakeholder review.

What can project teams do in DroneDeploy?

Depending on the scope, authorized users can review the orthomosaic, view geotagged photos, take distance and area measurements, navigate available 3D models, compare collection dates, and share approved deliverables with the broader team.

Can Aerial Photogrammetry support construction progress monitoring?

Yes. When recurring capture is included in the scope, photogrammetry can support Eagle’s Construction Progress Monitoring and Documentation service by creating a consistent, time-stamped visual record. Current conditions can be compared with earlier collections, reviewed alongside project plans, and shared with owners, contractors, engineers, and other participants.

When should Aerial LiDAR be used with Aerial Photogrammetry?

The two methods work well together when an assignment requires both high-resolution visual context and bare-earth terrain beneath vegetation. Photogrammetry provides detailed color imagery, while LiDAR contributes classified ground measurements and terrain modeling.
