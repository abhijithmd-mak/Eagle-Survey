Industrial, Manufacturing & Logistics

Industrial site surveys provide the existing-condition data, construction control, and record documentation required for plants, manufacturing facilities, warehouses, material yards, and logistics developments.

Who we work with

Operating facilities, active yards, and the record documentation that follows.

Industrial work rarely starts on clean ground. Existing structures, live operations, stockpiles and utility congestion all have to be documented before design begins and verified again as construction proceeds. Eagle Surveying combines conventional survey, aerial LiDAR, photogrammetry and terrestrial laser scanning to build one accurate picture of a working site.

We support industrial developers, plant and facility teams, logistics and distribution owners, civil engineers, general contractors, material producers, and operations teams. Volume and change-over-time reporting runs on a repeating schedule where material is moving, and scan-to-CAD data gives engineers usable existing geometry for retrofits and tie-ins.

Primary project services

Existing conditions, control, quantities, closeout.

Topographic Survey Existing-conditions base map of a working site, tied to survey control. Aerial LiDAR Mapping Classified point cloud and DTM across large yards, plants and vegetated tracts. Terrestrial Laser Scanning (TLS) Registered point clouds and scan-to-CAD of structural steel, tanks and MEP space. Volumetric & Stockpile Analysis Material quantities and cut-and-fill from repeat surface comparison. Construction Staking Layout for pads, racks, utilities and paving on active industrial sites. ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey Transaction survey for industrial acquisition, financing and portfolio work. Construction Progress Monitoring Time-stamped progress records across single sites or a program. As-Built Survey Record documentation of constructed locations and elevations at closeout.
