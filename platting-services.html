What it is

Lots, blocks, easements and dedications — on the record.

Platting is the process of creating an official map that divides, combines, or reconfigures land into legally recognized lots, blocks, easements, rights-of-way, and public dedications. The plat is reviewed by the applicable city, county, or other reviewing authority and, once approved, is recorded in the county real-property records.

Depending on the property and jurisdiction, the process may include preliminary plats, final plats, replats, amending plats, minor plats, conveyance plats, or other locally defined plat types. Requirements and review procedures vary substantially among Texas jurisdictions, and Eagle Surveying prepares plats for projects across the DFW metroplex, Central Texas, and throughout Texas.

How Eagle runs it

Fewer review cycles, because fewer avoidable comments.

Eagle Surveying prepares the survey components of the plat, coordinates with the owner, civil engineer, architect, utilities, and reviewing authority, responds to survey-related review comments, and supports the project through approval and recordation. Our objective is to reduce avoidable review issues and maintain clear coordination throughout the process.
