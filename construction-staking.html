What it is

Lines, grades and elevations, put on the ground.

Construction Staking, also called construction layout, transfers the locations, lines, grades, and elevations shown on signed and sealed plans prepared by the appropriate licensed design professionals into physical points and marks in the field.

Eagle Surveying calculates the requested layout from the approved plans and digital design files, establishes or verifies project control, and stakes the work in the sequence requested by the contractor. Layout may include offsets, cut-and-fill information, centerlines, building lines, utility locations, finished grades, and other construction control appropriate to the scope. Our crews perform construction staking with Trimble robotic total stations across the DFW metroplex, Central Texas, and all of Texas.

How Eagle runs it

Crews that keep up with the schedule, not the other way around.

Our project managers coordinate with contractors, engineers, owners, and superintendents regarding mobilization, phasing, revisions, and field conditions, so layout arrives when the work in front of it is ready.

01 Mobilization Scheduling coordinated with the superintendent so crews stake ahead of the work.
02 Phasing Layout sequenced to the contractor’s phases rather than delivered all at once.
03 Revisions Plan revisions tracked and reflected in subsequent layout.
04 Field conditions Conditions in the field communicated back to the contractor, engineer, or owner.

What is construction staking?+

Also called construction layout, it transfers the locations, lines, grades, and elevations shown on signed and sealed plans prepared by the appropriate licensed design professionals into physical points and marks in the field.

What do you need to start?+

The approved plans and digital design files. Eagle Surveying calculates the requested layout from them, then establishes or verifies project control before staking.

Can you stake the work in phases?+

Yes. Layout is staked in the sequence requested by the contractor, and our project managers coordinate mobilization, phasing, and revisions with the construction team.

What happens if the plans conflict?+

Apparent plan conflicts, missing information, or design questions are referred to the appropriate design professional for clarification rather than resolved through unauthorized design changes.
