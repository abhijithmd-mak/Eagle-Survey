The method

Two surfaces, one number your estimators can stand behind.

Volumetric and stockpile analysis compares surface models captured from the air to compute cut, fill, net volume, and material quantities across a site. By flying a site on a defined schedule and comparing each surface to a prior survey or design surface, we produce the data your estimators and project engineers use to verify quantities, track inventory, and compare existing conditions to design surfaces.

On sites where material volume drives contract value, full-coverage aerial measurement provides a consistent basis for quantity calculations across the entire site.

What the number is based on

Reported quantities are based on the agreed measurement limits, base surface, comparison surface, and other project assumptions.

Eagle Surveying does not determine shrink, swell, compaction, or other material-conversion factors. Our accurate survey-grade surface data provides engineers, estimators, and project teams with a reliable foundation for quantity calculations, inventory tracking, and earthwork analysis.
