Survey-Grade Quantities for Earthwork and Inventory.

What It Is

Measured Surfaces Turned Into Usable Quantities.

Volumetric and stockpile analysis compares surface models captured from the air to compute cut, fill, net volume, and material quantities across a site. By flying a site on a defined schedule and comparing each surface to a prior survey or design surface, we produce the data your estimators and project engineers use to verify quantities, track inventory, and compare existing conditions to design surfaces. On sites where material volume drives contract value, full-coverage aerial measurement provides a consistent basis for quantity calculations across the entire site.
