The data

What the eye cannot see, measured across a whole roof.

Aerial thermal imagery uses a radiometric thermal sensor on a UAS to capture temperature data across a site, roof, or asset, delivered as radiometric imagery and thermal orthomosaics where every pixel carries a temperature value. Thermal data reveals patterns that the naked eye and standard cameras cannot, including temperature variations that may indicate moisture intrusion, heat loss, and other anomalies across large or hard-to-access surfaces, captured quickly and without putting anyone on a roof or near live equipment.

We capture thermal imagery with our quad UAS platforms during a single mobilization, providing datasets from one site visit. Eagle Surveying provides aerial thermal data capture throughout Texas, including the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex and Central Texas, with select assignments supported outside the state.

Nobody walks the roof and nobody stands near live equipment — the sensor flies it instead.

What is aerial thermal imaging?+

Aerial thermal imaging uses a radiometric thermal sensor mounted on a UAS to capture temperature data across roofs, facilities, solar arrays, equipment areas, and other accessible assets. Deliverables may include georeferenced radiometric imagery and thermal orthomosaics in which each pixel contains temperature information. The data can help qualified engineers, consultants, inspectors, and analysts identify patterns or anomalies requiring further evaluation.

Does Eagle Surveying interpret or diagnose thermal conditions?+

No. Eagle Surveying captures, processes, georeferences, and delivers thermal data, but we do not diagnose moisture intrusion, heat loss, equipment failure, roof condition, or other thermal causes. The client's engineer, roofing consultant, building-envelope specialist, inspector, or other qualified professional interprets the information and determines whether additional investigation or corrective action is appropriate.
