What It Captures

Radiometric Data: Temperature Information in Every Pixel.

A radiometric sensor records temperature information at every pixel. Eagle processes and georeferences overlapping captures in DroneDeploy to produce a thermal orthomosaic, giving your team both the radiometric source imagery and a continuous mapped view of recorded temperature patterns.

Per-Pixel Values

Each radiometric pixel retains temperature information from the captured image.

Mapped Context

Processed and georeferenced imagery places the recorded temperature information across the documented asset.

Broader Coverage

Aerial collection documents variation across large, elevated, or difficult-to-access areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Project Teams Ask About Aerial Thermal Imagery.

What is Aerial Thermal Imaging?

Aerial thermal imaging uses a radiometric thermal sensor mounted on a UAS to capture temperature data across roofs, facilities, solar arrays, equipment areas, and other accessible assets. Deliverables may include georeferenced radiometric imagery and thermal orthomosaics in which each pixel contains temperature information. The data can help qualified engineers, consultants, inspectors, and analysts identify patterns or anomalies requiring further evaluation.

Does Eagle Surveying interpret or diagnose thermal conditions?

No. Eagle Surveying captures, processes, georeferences, and delivers thermal data, but we do not diagnose moisture intrusion, heat loss, equipment failure, roof condition, or other thermal causes. The client’s engineer, roofing consultant, building-envelope specialist, inspector, or other qualified professional interprets the information and determines whether additional investigation or corrective action is appropriate.
