Reality Capture

A Complete View, From Air to Ground.

Eagle operates a dedicated Reality Capture department that combines advanced aerial and ground-based technology with professional survey control. From large tracts of land to buildings, structures, and active construction sites, our team captures the information project teams need to understand existing conditions, coordinate work, document progress, and plan what comes next.

Every assignment is scoped around the decisions the data must support. That determines the collection method, required control, level of detail, file formats, and final deliverables. Survey-grade datasets are tied to established control and reviewed under Texas RPLS oversight. The result is data, imagery, models, and documentation that support real project decisions.

NDAA Compliant | Blue UAS Approved

Cleared Platforms for Projects With Higher Requirements.

Some projects require more than standard aerial collection. Eagle fields qualifying aircraft and sensor configurations that are NDAA compliant and Blue UAS approved, supporting assignments with elevated procurement, security, and site-access requirements.

Multiple Platform Options A range of aircraft allows the collection plan to be matched to site size, sensor needs, endurance, and operating conditions. NDAA compliance and Blue UAS approval apply to qualifying airframe and sensor configurations, not to every platform in the fleet.
Mission Planning & Site Coordination Airspace, access, owner protocols, required documentation, and data-handling expectations are addressed before mobilization.
Survey Integration When measurement is part of the scope, aerial collection remains connected to Eagle’s survey control, production workflow, and professional review.

Flight operations are conducted under FAA Part 107 by pilots holding FAA Remote Pilot Certificates.

Surveying Services

One Surveying Partner Across the Project Lifecycle.

A land title survey shapes the transaction. Topographic data drives design. Platting establishes the legal framework. Construction staking transfers approved plans to the field. As-built documentation records what was completed.

Eagle keeps those phases connected. The records, control, calculations, and project history established early in the process carry forward as the work advances. Each deliverable supports the next decision, and one firm remains accountable for the surveying throughout the project.

All surveying services
ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey→ Boundaries, easements, improvements, and title conditions for due diligence and financing. Category 1A Land Title Survey→ Texas-specific boundary and title documentation for real estate transactions. Category 1B Standard Land Survey→ Commonly known as a boundary survey, establishing or retracing legal boundaries. Topographic Survey→ Elevations, contours, drainage, structures, and visible utilities for design. Tree Survey→ Location and size data for existing trees supporting design and municipal requirements. Platting Services→ Preliminary plats, final plats, replats, and amending plats creating legal lots. Construction Staking→ Signed and sealed design plans translated into precise field layout. Form Board Survey→ Form location and elevation documented before concrete is placed. As-Built Survey→ Final locations and elevations of completed improvements for closeout. Easement Exhibits & Legal Descriptions→ Signed and sealed exhibits defining easement location, dimensions, and purpose. Elevation Certificate→ Building elevations relative to mapped flood-hazard data for permitting and insurance.
