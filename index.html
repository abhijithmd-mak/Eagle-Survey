Reality Capture

The site, captured once. Measured for years.

Seven services, one department, our own crews and fleet — every dataset tied to survey control.

Aerial LiDAR Mapping

Dense, survey-controlled point clouds that capture the bare-earth surface beneath vegetation — delivered as terrain models, contours, and CAD-ready surfaces from NDAA-compliant and Blue UAS Cleared platforms.

Aerial Photogrammetry

Survey-accurate, measurable site data from overlapping high-resolution drone imagery — orthomosaics, terrain and surface models, 3D models, and contours, processed in DroneDeploy with direct client access.

Terrestrial Laser Scanning (TLS)

Millimeter-level capture of buildings, facilities, and structures — delivered as georeferenced point clouds and scan-to-CAD/BIM data tied to project control for design, coordination, and verification.

360° Site Documentation & Virtual Walkthroughs

Immersive, time-stamped 360° imagery pinned to your plans so any stakeholder can walk the site virtually and see it as it existed on a given date. Hosted in DroneDeploy with direct client access.

Aerial Thermal Imagery

Drone-based radiometric thermal capture across roofs, facilities, and hard-to-access assets — delivered as georeferenced thermal orthomosaics. Eagle captures the data and delivers it; your experts interpret it.

Construction Progress Monitoring and Documentation

Recurring aerial and 360° ground capture on a defined schedule builds a consistent, time-stamped visual timeline of your project from preconstruction through closeout. One source of truth, hosted in DroneDeploy.

Volumetric & Stockpile Analysis

Aerial surface models computed into cut, fill, and material quantities with consistent, repeatable, full-coverage measurement. Built for earthwork tracking, stockpile inventory, and pay-application support.
