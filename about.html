Company overview

Traditional surveying expertise, advanced spatial data technology.

Eagle Surveying is a professional land surveying and Reality Capture company headquartered in Texas with multiple offices serving projects statewide. Founded in 2016, the firm provides comprehensive surveying services to commercial developers, civil engineering firms, title companies, municipal agencies, and institutional clients across Texas.

Eagle Surveying is registered with the Texas Board of Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors as TX Firm #10194177 and led by three Registered Professional Land Surveyors who maintain direct oversight of every project delivered. The firm operates at the intersection of traditional land surveying expertise and advanced spatial data technology, with a large professional staff including licensed surveyors, senior project managers, experienced field crews, and specialized Reality Capture personnel.

Our approach

We manage every project with that urgency.

Every project begins with a clear understanding of scope, schedule, and deliverable requirements. Our project managers coordinate directly with clients, engineers, and stakeholders to ensure that survey data integrates seamlessly into the broader project workflow.

Design teams cannot proceed without topographic data. Transactions cannot close without ALTA surveys. Construction cannot advance without staking.

In the field, our crews operate Trimble GNSS and robotic total station systems along with our full suite of Reality Capture technology. Our office production team processes data through Trimble Business Center, Carlson, DroneDeploy, and LP360, delivering final products in the CAD formats and coordinate systems our clients specify.

Across the project lifecycle

One firm from acquisition through closeout.

Phase 01 Planning & acquisition ALTA/NSPS surveys, boundary surveys, and topographic surveys for site evaluation and transactional due diligence.
Phase 02 Design & entitlement Platting exhibits, easement descriptions, tree surveys, and regulatory documentation.
Phase 03 Construction Staking crews provide precise layout while our Reality Capture team delivers progress documentation and volumetric measurement.
Phase 04 Completion As-built surveys, elevation certificates, and closeout documentation.
Quality & compliance

Every deliverable, under an RPLS.

All surveying services are performed in accordance with the Texas Society of Professional Surveyors Manual of Practice and the ALTA/NSPS Minimum Standard Detail Requirements, where applicable. Every deliverable is prepared under the direct supervision of a Registered Professional Land Surveyor and reviewed through our internal quality control process before certification.

Texas requires surveying firms offering land surveying services to the public to register and employ an RPLS full-time. Eagle Surveying's firm registration is reflected in filed municipal plat documents as #10194177, providing verifiable proof of compliance.

Safety commitment

Built to meet contractor and agency requirements.

Eagle Surveying maintains an active Health, Safety, and Environmental program designed to meet the requirements of national general contractors, energy providers, and municipal agencies. The firm maintains compliance with ISNetworld and Veriforce reporting platforms and utilizes DISA, Inc. as a third-party administrator for drug and alcohol screening.
