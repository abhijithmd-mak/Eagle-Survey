When should a developer hire a land surveyor?+

A developer should involve a land surveyor early enough to support acquisition and due diligence before major design decisions are made. Surveying is commonly needed before closing, during site evaluation, before civil design, during platting and entitlement, before and throughout construction, and at closeout. Early coordination helps identify the correct survey product, title information, control, deliverables, and schedule before they become critical-path issues.

What is a commercial site survey?+

Commercial site survey is a broad search term rather than one standardized survey category. Depending on the project, the required scope may be an ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey, Category 1A survey, boundary survey, topographic survey, tree survey, plat, construction staking, easement exhibit, as-built survey, or a combination. Eagle Surveying reviews the transaction, design, permitting, and construction requirements to identify the appropriate commercial land surveying services.

What is a commercial due diligence survey?+

Commercial due diligence survey is a general term for survey information used to evaluate a property before acquisition, financing, or development. It is not one universal survey type. The required scope may include an ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey, Category 1A Land Title Survey, boundary survey, topographic survey, floodplain information, aerial mapping, or other project-specific work. Eagle Surveying helps identify the correct deliverables from the title, lender, design, and transaction requirements.

What surveying services are commonly needed for commercial development?+

Commercial development in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, Central Texas, and across the state may require ALTA/NSPS Land Title Surveys, Category 1A Land Title Surveys, boundary work, topographic surveys, tree surveys, platting, easement exhibits and legal descriptions, construction staking, form board surveys, as-built surveys, elevation certificates, and Reality Capture. The exact combination depends on the acquisition, entitlement, design, construction, and closeout requirements of the property and reviewing jurisdiction.

What industries use Reality Capture?+

Reality Capture is used across commercial development, industrial facilities, data centers, construction, civil engineering, architecture, transportation, utilities, energy, solar, municipal infrastructure, higher education, and public works. It is especially useful when a project requires detailed existing-condition data, broad site coverage, remote visibility, repeat documentation, dense measurements, or coordination among multiple design and construction teams.

Can drone surveys replace traditional surveying?+

Drone surveys can improve efficiency and provide dense coverage over large or difficult sites, but they generally complement rather than replace traditional land surveying. Ground control, boundary determinations, legal surveys, construction staking, monument recovery, and other licensed surveying activities still require professional survey methods and oversight. Eagle Surveying combines aerial data collection with Trimble GNSS, robotic instrumentation, field verification, and Registered Professional Land Surveyor oversight when the project requires a survey deliverable.

How do I choose between LiDAR, photogrammetry, terrestrial laser scanning, and 360° documentation?+

Aerial LiDAR is generally best for terrain, vegetation, large tracts, and corridor-style projects. Photogrammetry is well suited for visible surfaces, high-resolution imagery, orthomosaics, and open construction sites. Terrestrial laser scanning provides dense close-range measurements of buildings, structures, industrial facilities, and congested spaces. 360° documentation is intended for visual records and virtual walkthroughs rather than survey-grade measurement. Some projects benefit from combining multiple methods.

Do I need a licensed land surveyor for drone mapping in Texas?+

If drone data will be used as an authoritative representation of property boundaries, easements, rights-of-way, elevations tied to project control, or other matters regulated as professional land surveying in Texas, the work must be performed under the responsible charge of a Registered Professional Land Surveyor. A drone operator alone cannot certify survey deliverables. Eagle Surveying pairs its Reality Capture capabilities with licensed survey control, field verification, and RPLS oversight whenever the project requires a survey deliverable, which protects the engineer's design basis and the owner's approvals downstream.

Eagle Surveying provides licensed land surveying services throughout Texas, with offices in Denton and Waco supporting the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, North Texas, Central Texas, and statewide projects. Reality Capture services are available throughout Texas and for select assignments nationwide. Project location, scope, mobilization, airspace, access, and deliverable requirements are reviewed before confirming service availability and schedule.
