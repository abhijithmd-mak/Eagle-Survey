What this work demands

One window, one crew, one chance to capture it.

Venue and event work is defined by its constraints rather than its acreage. A stadium floor is available for a few hours between events. An airport apron has operational restrictions. A high-rise site sits in live urban traffic. The survey has to be planned around access, coordinated with operations and security, and executed in the window given.

Eagle Surveying brings a full Reality Capture department to that problem: terrestrial laser scanning for interior and structural geometry, 360° documentation for walkthrough records, and aerial capture where airspace and permissions allow. Coordination and documentation discipline matter more here than raw coverage.

Applicable services

Capture methods that fit a closed window.

Terrestrial Laser Scanning (TLS) Interior, structural and architectural geometry at millimeter level. 360° Site Documentation Navigable walkthrough records of the venue as it stood that day. Aerial Photogrammetry Measurable imagery and 3D models where airspace permits. Topographic Survey Site topography for temporary and permanent improvements. Construction Staking Layout inside constrained, access-controlled sites. As-Built Survey Record documentation of what was installed or built.
